# Query 1 – Daily Business Summary

## Business Question

How is the business performing each day?

## Metrics Used

- Total Orders
- Revenue
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Paid Orders
- Cancelled Orders
- Yesterday's Revenue
- Revenue Difference

## Business Interpretation

This report provides a daily summary of business performance by showing the total number of orders, total revenue generated, average order value, paid orders, and cancelled orders. It also compares each day's revenue with the previous day's revenue, helping the business identify increasing or decreasing sales trends.
